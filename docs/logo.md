# Logo brief

Status: needs decision.

## Current logo

`icons/logo.svg`: a white rounded rectangle (the pouch) with a dotted outline,
on a black rounded square. It reads as a generic pill or button, not as a pouch,
and has no colour.

## Constraints

- Must read clearly at 32 px (browser tab) and 180 px (iPhone home screen).
- iOS adds its own rounded corners, so the PNGs should be full squares with no
  transparent edges.
- One SVG source; all PNGs are rendered from it.

## Questions to answer

- Style: flat icon, or something with depth or texture?
- Colour: keep black and white, or add an accent colour (also used in the app)?
- Subject: the pouch itself, a tin, a counter or progress ring, or a letter mark?
- Any app icons you like the look of (for example the coffee app)?
